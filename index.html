Our Story

How a simple observation became a movement

Bridge began as the Bridge Sign Language Club at Lideta Catholic Cathedral School in 2025. What started as a student-led initiative trained over 100 students and graduated 41 in its first cohort, becoming the foundation for a broader educational network.

Founder's Message

Building Bridges Through Education

"Every meaningful change begins with a single step. Bridge was created to remove barriers, open doors, and give people opportunities to learn skills that enrich both their own lives and the lives of others. Our vision extends beyond teaching courses. We are building a community where education, culture, service, creativity, and inclusion come together. Whether you join us as a student, instructor, volunteer, or partner, you become part of a mission to build stronger communities through learning."
Abenezer Admasu Tinsae
Founder & President, Bridge Education Network
