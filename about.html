About Us

Who we are, and how we got here

Who We Are

A youth-led network for inclusive education

Bridge Education Network is a youth-led educational and community organization dedicated to creating inclusive learning opportunities, developing practical skills, and connecting communities through education and service.

The organization began as the Bridge Sign Language Club (BSLC) at Lideta Catholic Cathedral School, founded by Abenezer Admasu Tinsae. What started as a student initiative to promote communication between hearing and deaf communities grew into a broader vision of creating accessible education, inclusion, and community engagement.

Through our programs, we aim to empower individuals with knowledge, skills, and opportunities while promoting understanding and collaboration across communities.

Our Story

How a simple observation became a movement

Many deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals experience isolation because communication barriers limit their participation in schools, churches, and communities. Bridge began as an answer to that gap.

OriginsThe Spark

Founder Abenezer Admasu Tinsae first learned sign language through his involvement in church service and interpretation. Seeing the importance of communication and inclusion inspired him to share this skill with other students.

2025The Founding

The Bridge Sign Language Club was established at Lideta Catholic Cathedral School as a student-led initiative. The club started by registering approximately 100 interested students and provided a one-year sign language training program, despite challenges such as limited resources, teaching materials, and organizational difficulties.

Cohort OneThe Outcome

The first cohort became the foundation for expanding Bridge into a broader educational and inclusion network:

  • 41 students successfully completed the program
  • A graduation ceremony recognizing student achievements
  • Student testimonials and documented impact
  • The establishment of the first BSLC Alumni Network
