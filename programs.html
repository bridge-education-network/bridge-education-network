Beginner

  • Learn Ethiopian Sign Language from the ground up
  • Build a strong vocabulary and understand the fundamentals of deaf communication
  • Practice everyday conversations and basic expressions

Intermediate

  • Improve conversational fluency
  • Learn grammar, facial expressions, and practical communication skills
  • Build confidence through real-life situations and interactive practice

Advanced

  • Master fluent communication
  • Develop interpretation and public communication skills
  • Learn professional ethics, leadership, and career opportunities in sign language

About the Program

Professional Ethiopian Sign Language training from beginner to advanced levels, helping bridge communication between Deaf and hearing communities. Guided by our "The Eyes Can Hear" model, we believe communication has the power to remove barriers, strengthen communities, and create equal opportunities for everyone.

Reading Division(የቁጥር ትምህርት)

Helps students develop confidence and accuracy in reading the sacred texts of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.

  • Hymn of Mary(ውዳሴ ማርያም,መልክአ ማርያም, መልክአ ኢየሱስ)
  • Book of Psalms(መዝሙረ ዳዊት)
  • Old Testament Reading(ብሉያት ንባብ)
  • New Testament Reading(ሐዲሳት ንባብ)
  • Miracle Books(የተአምራት,ገድላት መፃህፍት)
  • Other Liturgical Texts(ልዩ ልዩ መልክአ መልኮች)

Melody Division(የዜማ ትምህርት)

Introduces students to the liturgical music tradition of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church.

  • Wudase Mariam Melodies(ውዳሴ ማርያም,መስተጋብእ)
  • Liturgical Melodies(ግብረ ዲቁና ወይም ለምእመናን ትንጥኦ)
  • Seatat ሰዓታት ( መልክአ ሥእል, ተፈስሒ ማርያም, እሴብህ ጸጋኪ, መልክአ ውዳሴ ..)

About the Program

Traditional Ethiopian Orthodox reading and liturgical education across two divisions, Reading and Melody. Our instruction follows the authentic teachings, discipline, and liturgical practices of the Church while making learning accessible to students of different ages and backgrounds.
